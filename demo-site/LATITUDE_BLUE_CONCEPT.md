# Latitude blue-shell product concept

This unofficial concept is a separate design variant derived from the existing white Latitude demo. No changes were made to Vantor or the white Latitude project.

- White demo branch: demo/latitude-concept-2026
- Blue-shell branch: demo/latitude-blue-shell-2026
- Source of the logo: user-uploaded Latitude icon and wordmark, vectorized as white transparent SVGs to remain legible against the brand-blue sidebar.
- Main page: /demo
- Everything is simulated and browser-local. No bank or payment APIs are connected.
