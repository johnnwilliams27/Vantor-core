Views listed by category (Treasury, Activity, Compliance, Yield, Custom). Click to drill into a full-screen view with chart, filters, and export. Cleaner overview, more room per view. Custom views get their own section with a distinct accent.

C. Pinned + Browse Hybrid

Top section shows pinned views (KPI banner + 2-3 favorite charts rendered inline). Below, a compact list of all views with quick actions (fork, export, drill-in). Users pin their most-used views for at-a-glance monitoring, browse the rest in the list. "Generate Report" composes pinned views into a PDF.
